Our Corporate Journey

Discover our industrial background, milestones, and how our specialized engineering skills evolved over the years.

2001

The Inception

LOGIQUA Automation is established by co-founders Gilbert BAUER and Mathieu GEHIN. For an initial decade, we focus purely on automation programming execution, developing robust hands-on industrial site expertise.

2011

The Strategic Venture

To deepen our electrical design framework and secure full hardware provisioning capacities for clients, we team up with Fabrice DEBS to establish the standalone legal entity DELTA-Fil.

Today

Parallel Group Trajectory

Since the joint alliance, both entities advance side by side. LOGIQUA Automation concentrates on core electrical engineering, overall project management, and PLC development, while DELTA-Fil handles hardware panel assembly and fields extensive wiring operations across multiple sectors.

A Holistic Vision

The close cooperation between our automation engineering desk and dedicated fieldwork teams yields unparalleled corporate responsiveness, taking care of projects safely from structural drafts down to production startup.

LOGIQUA Automation

Our engineering division relies on a skilled 5-person team driving project management, schematic design, and code programming.

DELTA-Fil
